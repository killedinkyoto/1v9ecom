The Problem

The crash isn't a
character flaw.

01

Coffee gives you forty good minutes, then bills you for them.

The first cup lands and you feel it. Sharp, online, ready to go. Forty, fifty minutes later the slope starts. So you have another, and it does a little less than the last one. Somewhere in there the caffeine stopped being the thing that gets you working and became the thing you need just to feel normal.

02

Past a certain point, more caffeine only makes you twitchy.

The work you do punishes overstimulation. You can't trace a bug or review a model while your leg's bouncing and your chest is doing that thing. There's a kind of "alert" that's really just anxious, and the fourth coffee lives there.

03

The 3pm version of your brain is the worst one you own.

Not because you're lazy. Because the stimulant ran out and all the fatigue it was holding back shows up at once. You spend the back half of the day on fumes, doing the shallow busywork that feels like work and ships nothing.

The usual advice is sleep more, drink less coffee, try meditating. Fine advice, none of it touches the real problem. You've been using a tool built for a quick jolt to hold a long shift. Those are two different jobs, and most people in your line of work feel the gap every single afternoon.
