## SECTION 12 Objections / FAQ  *(folds the angle doc's skepticism-destroyer + the subscription-trap pillar)*

**Eyebrow:** THE ONES YOU'RE ACTUALLY ASKING

> **Is this just cheap caffeine behind a proprietary blend?**
> No, and you can confirm it in ten seconds. Every milligram is printed on the tub. 300mg Alpha-GPC, 600mg Inositol, 100mg L-theanine, 200mg green tea caffeine, the full B-complex, all of it. When a brand hides the formula behind a "focus matrix," it's usually cheap caffeine and a decorative dusting of the expensive stuff. There's nothing to hide here, so we don't.

> **I've tried caffeine and theanine before and didn't feel much. Why is this different?**
> Two reasons. The choline and the dose. A plain caffeine-theanine pill skips the choline donor, so when you fire up your processing speed you burn through your acetylcholine and end up awake but empty. The 300mg of Alpha-GPC is the part that fills that gap. And a powder in cold water absorbs faster and more evenly than a hard capsule sitting in your stomach.

> **Will it make me feel wired or robotic, like a prescription stimulant?**
> No. Prescription stimulants flood your dopamine and can flatten you out, which is the opposite of what creative work needs. This is calm alertness, not vibration. You won't feel like you're buzzing in your chair. You'll notice you looked up and three hours had gone by.

> **What does it taste like? Is it full of chemical sweetener?**
> Cold grape juice, crisp and light, made to sip through a long morning on an empty stomach without going sour. No heavy syrup, no dye, none of the cloying pre-workout aftertaste.

> **Am I going to get trapped in a subscription I can't cancel?**
> No, and this one matters to us, because it's happened to all of us. You can buy a single tub with no subscription at all. If you do subscribe and save, cancelling is one click, stated plainly, no chatbot maze, no ten confirmation screens. We're not going to make leaving harder than joining.

> **Do I have to give up coffee?**
> You don't. Plenty of people keep the morning cup and just stop needing the second and third. It's not a detox, it's a steadier morning. Most people drift down on their own once the mornings stop being a fight.

> **How fast will I notice?**
> The calm-alertness part, day one, inside the first half hour. The steadier-mornings part is something most people clock in the first week, usually the third or fourth time they realize they got into the work without the usual fight.
