## WHY THESE THREE (the strategy, for you, not the page)

The dossiers name all three orbits of competitor this ICP knows:
- **Magic Mind** is the direct "productivity drink" positioning rival and the one Julian has most likely actually bought. It's the cleanest contrast: it's a *proprietary blend* (his single biggest trust objection), only 55mg caffeine in the Original (so it under-delivers on the morning engine he needs), and runs $2-3 a shot. MINDOR wins on transparency and on being a real morning dose, not on "more is better."
- **Thesis** is the "personalized nootropic" he's seen all over podcasts and IG. It's capsules not a drink (misses the desk-ritual behavioral cue the angle doc flagged), the blends don't disclose per-ingredient amounts, every blend contains caffeine anyway, and it's $79-119/mo on a subscription. MINDOR wins on transparency, format, and the no-subscription-trap pillar.
- **Mushroom Coffee (Ryze)** is the category the avatar dossier explicitly ridicules ("tastes worse than the dirt coffee I drank in Bosnia," gritty). It's a proprietary six-mushroom blend, low caffeine (~48-90mg), no choline, no theanine, and the taste/texture is its known failure mode. MINDOR wins on taste, on actually being a focus formula rather than a coffee swap, and on transparency.

The through-line that makes the table land for *this* buyer: **all three hide their doses in a blend, and MINDOR is the only column where he can read every number.** That's belief #5 (verifiable authority of the label) doing the closing work. Note I did NOT claim MINDOR is "stronger" or "better dosed" across the board, because that's the claim that gets fact-checked and lost. The honest, winnable claims are: transparency, real morning caffeine dose, a named choline source, a drinkable format, and no subscription trap. Those are all true and all verifiable.
